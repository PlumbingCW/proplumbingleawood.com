Older homes, older pipes in Prairie Village

The city's own website is celebrating 75 years of Prairie Village, which tells you that much of its housing dates to the middle of the last century. Homes of that age often still have original cast iron or clay drains and galvanized or early copper supply lines. Those materials corrode, crack and let roots in, and the problems show up as repeat clogs, slow drains and pinhole leaks.

The practical point is to look before you replace. A sewer camera inspection shows what a mid-century drain line looks like now, and it costs far less than guessing. When the camera shows damage, sewer line repair covers lining, bursting and replacement.
