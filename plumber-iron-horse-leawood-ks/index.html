The repairs that fill a typical week

Many calls from established communities are small and annoying. A faucet drips, a toilet runs, a disposal jams or a shutoff valve will not close. These are quick fixes when diagnosed well and slow ones when they are patched blindly. We handle faucet repair, toilet repair and garbage disposal work, and we replace worn shutoff valves while we are there.

When the whole house is the question

If several fixtures show the same problem, low pressure, noise or discolored water, the cause may be upstream. We test the pressure at an outside spigot first, then look at the visible pipe. A single slow drain is often just a clog, and drain cleaning clears it.

Johnson County Wastewater says discharging a sump pump into the sanitary sewer is against code. If your home has a pit, confirm the discharge goes outside.
