Privacy Policy

This page explains what information we handle when you visit this website or call our line.

Call (866) 310-6010 now

Phone calls

Our business runs on phone calls. When you call, we ask for your name, address and a description of the problem so we can send the right plumber. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Website visits

This website has no forms and does not ask you to type anything in. Like most sites, the server and the hosting provider may log basic technical data such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested.

How we use information

We use call information to arrange service, answer your questions and keep records of work. We do not use it for other purposes.

Sharing

We share the details of your job with the plumber assigned to it. We may also share information when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask what we have about you, or ask us to correct it, by emailing contact@proplumbingleawood.com or calling (866) 310-6010.

Changes

We may update this policy, and the date of the latest change is November 12, 2026.
