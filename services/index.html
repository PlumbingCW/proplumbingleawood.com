Water where it should not be

Start here when water is spreading. A burst pipe means shut off the main and call, while a hidden leak or a high bill calls for leak detection. If the break is outside, water service line repair covers the pipe from the meter to the house, and a failed pump in a wet basement belongs to sump pump repair. Longer-term damp problems are covered under basement waterproofing.

Drains and sewer lines

A single slow fixture is a drain cleaning job. When several fixtures back up together, see sewer line cleaning, and for grease or roots that keep returning, hydro jetting cleans the pipe wall. A sewer camera inspection shows what is inside the line, and sewer line repair fixes a pipe that is cracked or collapsed.

Hot water

A tank that leaks or a heater that has gone cold may need water heater repair or water heater replacement. If you are planning ahead, water heater installation and tankless water heater service let you choose size and type. Scale from hard water affects all of them, and water softener and filtration covers treatment options.

Fixtures and upgrades

Smaller jobs are still urgent when water will not stop. See toilet repair and installation, faucet repair and garbage disposal repair. New work, such as added fixtures or appliance hookups, falls under plumbing installation, and aging supply pipes are the subject of repiping.

Businesses

Restaurants, offices and retail units can call about commercial plumbing, including after-hours emergencies.

Which pages are emergency pages

Pages for burst pipes, leaks, drains, sewer lines, water heater failures, toilets, faucets, disposals, sump pumps, service lines and commercial work are written for people with an active problem, and each one lists what to do while you wait. Planned work such as installation and repiping is written for people comparing options. Both kinds can use the same 24/7 line, and you hear a clear price before work starts. Same-day appointments are available when the schedule allows.
