What Town Center Plaza is

Wikipedia describes Town Center Plaza in Leawood as an open-air shopping center that opened in 1996. The plaza sits at Roe Avenue and 119th Street. This page is about businesses in the surrounding commercial area, not an affiliation with the plaza or its owners.

Commercial calls are not residential calls

A leak in a suite raises questions a house does not. Who owns the pipe, the landlord or the tenant? Is there a shared line serving several suites? Does the lease require a particular contractor, notice to management or an after-hours window? Check your lease and tell us what it says before work begins.

We handle commercial plumbing repairs, and we confirm the scope with whoever authorizes the work.

Restaurants and food service

1

Keep grease out

Fats and oils harden in lines. Wipe pans, scrape plates and keep a disposal from becoming a grease pipe.

2

Know your interceptor

If your kitchen has a grease interceptor, keep service records. A full one can back up the line.

3

Watch the floor drains

A slow floor drain is an early sign of a blockage. Call before it overflows.

4

Call with the details

Tell us which drain, how often it slows and whether it affects the dining room.

Drains that carry a lot

Retail and restaurant lines carry more than a house does, and a blockage can close a kitchen. We start with a camera look. Hydro jetting clears grease and scale from the pipe wall, and sewer line cleaning handles the main. A leak detection visit helps when a ceiling stains or a meter runs with the doors closed.

Restrooms and fixtures

Public restrooms see steady use. Running toilets, sticking flush valves and dripping faucets waste water and wear out parts. We handle toilet repair and faucet repair, and we can look at a pattern if the same restroom calls repeatedly.
