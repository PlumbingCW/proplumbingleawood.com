Plumbing Service Area: Leawood, KS and Nearby Towns

Leawood is the center of our dispatch area, and three neighboring cities are inside it. The 24/7 line covers all of them at any hour.

Call (855) 575-2890 now

Leawood, Kansas

Leawood is in Johnson County, and its ZIP codes are 66206, 66209 and 66211. All three are in our dispatch area. Our office is at 11111 Nall Ave, and Nall Avenue and Roe Avenue are the two main north-south arterials we use. State Line Road forms the eastern edge with Missouri, and I-435 crosses the southern part of the city.

We also work in the busy areas around Town Center Plaza at Roe Avenue and 119th Street, and Park Place near Nall Avenue and 117th Street. Businesses there can use commercial plumbing after hours. Ironwoods Park and Leawood City Park are well known greenspaces, and homes nearby face the same water and sewer rules as the rest of the city.

We also have pages for five parts of Leawood: Old Leawood, Leawood South, Hallbrook, Iron Horse and the Town Center Plaza area. None has an official boundary we could verify, so we confirm coverage from the street address.

Nearby towns with their own pages

Overland Park borders Leawood to the north and west, and we cover ZIP codes 66212, 66213, 66210 and 66224. Prairie Village is north of Leawood, with ZIP codes 66208, 66207 and 66206. Mission Hills is farther up State Line Road, using ZIP code 66208, with the 66205 area next door.

Each page describes what is different about that town's homes, water and sewer lines. Fairway, Roeland Park and Westwood are near Prairie Village, and Lenexa is near Overland Park. We take calls from them and confirm coverage by address.

Water and sewer, across all of these towns

WaterOne serves Leawood, Overland Park, Prairie Village, Mission Hills and 13 other cities, and it states that the customer-owned section of the water service line is the customer's responsibility. Johnson County Wastewater says homeowners maintain, repair and replace the private sewer service line. Both rules mean a break in your own section is yours to fix, and the first call is to a plumber.

How we confirm coverage

Coverage is decided by street address, not by the name on the mailing label. A few Leawood addresses carry an Overland Park or Prairie Village mailing city, and a few Overland Park streets are close to the Leawood line. That is why we ask for the address before we promise anything.

When you call, we check the address against our dispatch area and tell you the answer on the call. If an address falls outside the area, we say so plainly and suggest you ask your utility or a neighbor for a local referral.

What we need to know when you call

Give us the street address, the ZIP code, and the nearest cross street if the house is hard to find. Tell us what failed and whether water is still running. If you live in a gated community, a condominium or an apartment building, say so, because access may need a code or a call to the manager.

If you rent, the owner or manager often has to approve the work. Tell us that on the call so we can explain the next step.

Emergency calls across the area

The same line answers in every town on this page at any hour. A burst pipe in Overland Park and a sewage backup in Prairie Village get the same questions and the same shutoff advice. If you are in Mission Hills, older homes and mature trees often mean a camera look at the sewer line.

We book same-day appointments when the schedule allows. We do not promise a specific arrival time, because traffic, weather and other emergencies change it, and we would rather tell you the truth than a number that slips.

Planned work across the area

Water heater replacements, repiping, a new sump pump and a sewer camera inspection can all be booked ahead. Planned jobs are easier to price and easier to schedule around work and school. See all plumbing services for the full list, or call and describe what you see.

Who to call when it is not a plumbing problem

Some problems belong to someone else. A main water break in the street, low pressure on the whole block or a water quality question is a utility matter, and WaterOne is the place to start. A sewer main backup that affects several homes is a Johnson County Wastewater matter. A storm drain that floods the street goes to your city. If the problem is inside your property lines, call us.
