Emergency Hydro Jetting in Leawood, KS

When a main line backs up repeatedly, a cable only punches a hole through the blockage. Hydro jetting scours the whole pipe wall. Our 24/7 line is open now for Leawood, KS. If sewage is rising, stop using every fixture and keep people out of the wet area until we arrive.

What hydro jetting does

A hydro jetter sends water through a hose at several thousand psi, out of a nozzle that fires jets backward and forward. The forward jets cut through blockage, and the rear jets pull the hose along while they scrub the pipe wall clean. The result is a line that is actually clear, not a channel through the middle of a blockage.

That difference matters for grease, scale and root hair. A cable can leave a ring of buildup that catches the next load of waste in weeks. Jetting removes it.

When jetting is an emergency

A backed-up main line with sewage in a tub, shower or floor drain is a health and damage problem. Stop water use, keep children and pets away, and call. We take jetting emergencies at night and on weekends, and same-day appointments are available when the schedule allows.

Johnson County Wastewater runs a 24/7 line for sewer backups in the public main, and its website lists the number. If neighbors on your street are backing up too, report it to them, since the blockage may be in the public line rather than yours.

A drain cleaning visit with a cable is the right first step for ordinary clogs. When the same line fails every few months, jetting is what changes the pattern.

Why we look first

We run a camera before jetting a line we have not seen. High-pressure water can worsen a cracked, offset or collapsed pipe, and a camera shows which kind of line we have. If the pipe is damaged, jetting is not the answer, and sewer line repair is. The camera inspection also gives you video of the pipe.

Safety steps and standards for jetting

High pressure water can cut skin and damage pipe, so jetting belongs to trained technicians with the right nozzle and pressure for each pipe. Cleaning does not replace a repair on a cracked line. Johnson County Wastewater states that the homeowner owns the private line, so ask the technician to show you the camera footage before and after.

  • Stay clear of the cleanout while the hose is running.
  • Cover floor drains near the work.
  • Ask for the before and after camera footage.
  • Report any damage you notice right away.
