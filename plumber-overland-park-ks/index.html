Water and sewer service in Overland Park

WaterOne lists Overland Park among the 17 cities it serves, so drinking water comes from the same utility as Leawood. WaterOne states that the customer-owned section of the service line belongs to the customer to repair, replace and maintain. A leak between meter and house is therefore a plumbing call, and water service line repair covers it.

For sewers, Johnson County Wastewater states that homeowners are responsible for the private line from the house to the public main. If sewage backs up and neighbors are affected too, the public main may be the cause, and the utility runs a 24/7 line for that. If only your house is affected, our sewer line cleaning comes first, and sewer line repair follows if the camera shows damage.
